Terms & Payment Rules

Payments and earnings

Beat Hub automatically applies the platform commission to every successfully completed sale before earnings are added to the producer's available system balance.

Producers see their net earnings and the amount available for withdrawal. Platform commission records are handled separately within the Beat Hub system.

Withdrawals

Withdrawal requests are made to the producer's registered M-Pesa payout number, subject to successful payment processing and the platform's applicable payment rules.

Password and account security

Producers are responsible for keeping their account credentials secure. Password reset links are temporary and may only be used once.
