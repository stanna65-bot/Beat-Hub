Terms & Payment Rules

Beat and session payments

BeatHub applies its platform commission automatically to successful beat purchases and paid session bookings. Producers see the net amount credited to their system balance and available for withdrawal according to the platform rules.

Session booking

A selected session time is temporarily held while payment is pending. After successful payment the time becomes unavailable to other clients. Availability updates automatically.

Messages and time proposals

Messages can be used to discuss session details. A date or time mentioned in a message does not block the calendar. A proposed time must be explicitly confirmed and still available before the system updates the booking.

Password security

Password reset links are temporary and single-use.
