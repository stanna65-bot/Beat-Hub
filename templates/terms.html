Terms & Payment Rules

Beat Hub applies a 10% platform commission automatically to each completed sale.

The producer dashboard displays the producer's net earnings and available withdrawal balance. The commission is recorded internally in the platform ledger.

Example: KES 50,000 sale → KES 45,000 credited to producer → KES 5,000 platform commission.

Withdrawal requests are processed to the producer's registered M-Pesa payout number.
